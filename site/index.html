Moving to Panama in one paragraph

Panama uses the US dollar, has one of the region's more accessible retiree visas (the Pensionado, for a lifetime pension of about US$1,000 a month), and offers a choice of climates within a few hours' drive: hot cities and beaches, and cool mountain towns like Boquete. Most successful movers visit first, choose a region, pick a residency route with their own lawyer, arrange health insurance and banking early, and rent for six to twelve months before buying.
