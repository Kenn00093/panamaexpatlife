About Panama Expat Life

Panama Expat Life is a practical guide to moving to and living in Panama, written by a team that lives and works in Panama City, Pedasí, Boquete and beyond.

Why we built it

Most information about moving to Panama is either years out of date or written to sell something. We wanted one place that covers every region where expats actually live, is honest about the hard parts, and is checked against the current rules.

Editorial standards

  • Every guide shows the date it was last checked.
  • Rules that change often, such as residency requirements, are re-checked regularly against current published guidance and flagged clearly on the page.
  • We say when something is uncertain, and point you to a lawyer, doctor or tax adviser when a decision needs one.
  • Partners never pay to change what a guide says.

Disclosure

Panama Expat Life is run by a team affiliated with Panama Properties, a licensed Panamanian real estate brokerage that represents buyers. Where a guide mentions buying property, we may point readers to Panama Properties.

Some links on this site are referral partnerships with businesses such as lawyers, insurance brokers, movers and currency transfer services. If you use them, we may receive a fee, at no extra cost to you. We only list businesses we have vetted, and we label paid listings as such.

Nothing on this site is legal, tax, immigration, financial or medical advice. Always confirm important decisions with a qualified professional.
