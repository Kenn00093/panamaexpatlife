The Panama Relocation Guide

Everything you need to move to Panama, in one download: a step-by-step plan from leaving home to your first year, the real costs, and the lawyers, doctors, brokers, movers, builders and tradespeople we actually use, with their phone numbers.

You'll also get our free move checklist and new guides. Unsubscribe any time. See our privacy policy.

Why a paid guide?

This website covers the basics for free. The guide is what we'd hand a friend who's serious about moving: more than 40 chapters and about 90 pages, written by a team that lives in Panama City, Pedasí, Boquete and beyond.

  • Vetted local contacts in every chapter. Lawyers by region and specialty, the health insurance broker we use, private hospitals with phone numbers, movers, a pet relocation company, car dealers, mechanics, drivers, builders, architects, property managers, plumbers, electricians and more.
  • Real numbers. Monthly budgets for city, beach and highland living, a one-time moving budget, closing costs, taxes, bus fares and domestic flight prices.
  • The parts other guides skip. Leaving Canada or the US cleanly, wills and probate, hiring household help legally, keeping your residency, ageing in Panama and adjusting to life here.
  • Reference pages you'll keep using: a yearly calendar, a printable emergency sheet and a directory of every contact in the guide.
